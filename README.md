# Speak first, pay clearly: a Supernova AI case study

A product case study and working concept prototype by Hardik Jain.

- **Case study:** why Supernova's ₹9 trial, its paywall-first onboarding and its reliability issues are one broken first week, backed by 7,961 Google Play reviews, followed by a new flow, an A/B test plan and rollout.
- **Prototype:** click "Try the working prototype" to go through the new first week in Supernova's design language: First Win with Nova, an honest trial offer, a Day 2 reminder, one-tap cancel or refund, and non-blocking audio feedback.

Concept work, not affiliated with or endorsed by Supernova. No real payments or personal data are collected.
